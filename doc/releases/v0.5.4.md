## 中文

- W 周期从首次使用开始，与上一段结束独立计算；稳定周截止时间减七天反推起点，空闲滚动期限、首次采样及兑卡时刻不猜填起点。三分钟内期限抖动忽略。
- 历史行显示结束本行的重置方式。对应服务端 used 优先；没有用卡记录时，确认重置至少提前三十分钟归为提前重置，自然到期须有后续换窗证据；其余保留未知。最后剩余取本行结束前有效记录。
- 跨预计截止的采样空档，恢复后虽已换窗但不能确定结束方式时显示“自然重置(待确认)”。主行、详情和消费范围明确标注预计值；补证修正原事件，保留关闭状态，旧派生缓存自动更新。
- 修复空闲 deadline 连续变化后候选卡住、后续消费及再次重置未被接收的问题。获卡、到期和空闲 deadline 滚动不会独立切段。
- 统一周期行、详情、选择与多选、持续时间、Token 和金额的毫秒级半开范围。晚到证据更新选中范围并复用消费缓存；保留原始观察、事件对应关系和通知关闭状态。

## English

- Start W periods at first use inferred from a stable weekly deadline, independently of the previous closing event. Do not invent starts from rolling idle deadlines, sampling or redemption. Ignore deadline jitter within three minutes.
- Label rows by the event that ended them. Prefer a corresponding server use; otherwise classify confirmed recovery at least 30 minutes early as early. Natural expiry requires subsequent new-window evidence; insufficient evidence remains unknown. Final balances use the closing side.
- Show “Natural reset (unconfirmed)” when a sampling gap spans expiry and recovery proves a new window without establishing how or when the old one ended. Label estimated values in rows, details and consumption ranges. Late evidence refines the same event, preserves dismissal and updates older derived caches.
- Fix an idle-deadline candidate that could freeze later consumption and hide a subsequent reset. Grants, expiry and idle deadline movement do not independently split periods.
- Keep period rows, details, selection, multi-selection, duration, Token and cost queries on the same half-open millisecond ranges. Late evidence refreshes selected ranges using cached requests while preserving raw observations and dismissed notices.
